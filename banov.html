// WHAT AWAITS YOU

Built Different

Every system on NOCTURNIA is tuned to make survival feel brutal, rewarding, and genuinely sci-fi.

🗺️

A Massive World

256km² inspired by the real Banov District of Slovakia. 1 city, 35 villages, 2 water reservoirs, and 3 airports to explore.

🛸

The Alien War

Mysterious dwellings, hidden locations, and paranormal phenomena scattered across the map. Uncover the truth if you dare.

🕳️

Underground Complexes

4 large underground complexes, each rich with storylines, danger, and rewards that are crucial to progressing the map's narrative.

🤖

AI Companion

Equip a companion armband to summon an AI buddy that fights alongside you. Solo players finally have backup — if they can keep it alive.

⚔️

Deep Gear Progression

Layered gear tiers from vanilla loadouts to black-market sci-fi weapon packs. Progression that actually feels earned.

🌩️

Unforgiving Survival

Dense forests, swamps, and wild rivers with unpredictable weather. Sunny days can shift to storms without warning.

🏠

Build & Hide

Find safe locations to build your base or hide from the infected. The land rewards those who plan wisely.

💎

No Pay-to-Win

Donor gear options exist — but every single item is obtainable through gameplay. Money helps, grind does the same.
