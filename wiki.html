TerjeMedicine Survival Guide

Banov runs the TerjeMedicine overhaul, not the vanilla DayZ health system. Every illness and injury has its own stages, its own symptoms, and its own specific treatment — this page covers all of it, item by item.

// PHYSICAL CONDITION

Wounds & Trauma

Blunt hits, gunshots, blades, and blast damage each behave differently under TerjeMedicine. Hemostatic bandages, surgery kits, and specific medications all play a role.

Hematomas

In-game: Bruise (Hematoma)
Cause

Zombie hits or blunt weapon strikes. Multiple hematomas at once will start draining your health.

Recovery

Heals naturally over time.

Treatment

Ointments speed up healing: Viprosal, Capsicum, or Finalgon.

Gunshot, Stab & Internal Organ Wounds

In-game: Scratch / Deep Wound / Bullet Wound / Internal Organs Wound
Cause

Melee (stab) or firearms (bullet). Both cause severe bleeding and need immediate bandaging.

Symptoms

Hemostatic bandages control bleeding better than regular bandages or cloth. Even after bandaging, the wound can keep bleeding slightly until the foreign object (bullet/blade fragment) is removed via surgery. Severe cases become internal organ injuries — deeper and harder to treat.

Treatment

Surgical tools to remove the object: Medical Bowl w/ Instrument (Ceramic or White), Field Surgical Kit, IFAK, or AFAK. Higher-tier kits heal faster and more reliably.

Note

A well-maintained bulletproof vest can stop bullets outright (light vests stop small calibers only). A blocked bullet still leaves a hematoma even without penetration.

Surgical Sutures

In-game: Bandaged Wound (Clean/Dirty) / Sutured Wound (Clean/Dirty)
Use

Applied during surgery to close wounds after object removal.

Clean vs. Dirty

A bandaged or sutured wound stays in a "Clean" state as long as it's disinfected and monitored. Left dirty, it flips to a "Dirty" state that raises Sepsis risk sharply.

Note

Must be regularly disinfected — dirty sutures or dressings are a direct path to Sepsis.

Knockout / Coma

Critical state — server config dependent
Trigger

When health or blood drops critically low, instead of dying outright the player is knocked out and falls into a coma.

While down

A downed player can be revived by a teammate, or finished off by an enemy.

Revival

CPR, or a Defibrillator shock, brings blood and health back above the critical threshold and gets them back up (the shock itself also adds shock damage, so it's not risk-free).

Note

This system is a server-side toggle (Knockout-to-Coma and Medical Coma settings) — whether it's active on Banov depends on the current server config, not just the mod defaults.

Concussion

In-game: Contusion
Cause

A strong blow to the head from a blunt object, a nearby explosion, or a low-caliber bullet impact while wearing a ballistic helmet.

Symptoms

Headache, dizziness, unstable coordination, blurred vision — all hurt movement and combat effectiveness.

Treatment

Fades on its own, but Noopept (tablets) or Neirox (ampoule/injector) speed recovery.

Bleeding Rate (Hemostasis)

In-game: Fast Blood Clotting
What it does

Your body's natural process of stopping blood loss. Certain meds slow bleeding to buy time for further treatment.

Treatment

Vikasol (tablets) or Erytromixelin (injector).

Blood Regeneration (Hematopoiesis)

In-game: Blood Regeneration
What it does

Your body's process of rebuilding red blood cells after cuts, scratches, and bullet wounds.

Treatment

Irovit or Magnesium Sulfate (tablets), Erythropoetin (ampoule or injector), Erytromixelin (injector).

// INFECTIONS AND DISEASES

Diseases & Infections

Six distinct illnesses, each with its own cause, progression, and cure. Ignoring stage 1 almost always leads to a much worse stage 2 or 3.

Cold → Pneumonia

Cause

Prolonged exposure to cold temperatures.

Progression

Left untreated, a mild cold develops into pneumonia. Pneumonia causes slow, ongoing health loss.

Treatment

A mild cold can be cured just by resting in a warm place. Anything past that needs an antibiotic of matching level or higher — Tetracycline, Paracetamol, or Amibaktam for level 1; Ibuprofen, Nurofen, Amoxiclav, Piperacillin, Ketarol, or Amoxivan for level 2; Topoisomerase, Imipenem, Flemoclave, or Amoxiclav (injector) for level 3. Multiple doses are often needed.

Food Poisoning

In-game: Stomach Poison
Cause

Spoiled or undercooked food, rotten fruit, poisonous mushrooms, dirty water, or accidentally ingesting things like gasoline.

Symptoms

Nausea and vomiting hit fast. Left untreated it worsens into dehydration and gradual health loss.

Treatment

Drink plenty of water alongside medication. Level 1: Charcoal or Polysorb (tablets), Phthalazole. Level 2: Mesalazine, Chelating Tablets, Metoclopramid (ampoule). Level 3: Heptral (ampoule), Stomaproxidal (injector).

Chemical Poisoning

Cause

Inhaling toxic gases.

Symptoms

Starts as coughing, then nausea and vomiting as it worsens. Later stages cause severe, potentially fatal health loss without prompt treatment.

Treatment

Rombiopental (level 1 tablets), Neurocetal (level 2 ampoule), AntiChem Injector (level 3).

Zombie Virus

Cause

Airborne droplets and bodily fluids — small chance during close combat with infected, much higher if a zombie actually wounds you.

Symptoms

Long incubation period — you get over an hour of real time to find an antidote before it turns terminal. In the terminal stage, health declines rapidly.

Treatment

Zivirol, as an ampoule or injector. Note the antidote itself costs -20 health / -10 blood / -100 shock on injection, so use it before you're already critical.

Prevent

A face mask and ballistic armor reduce infection risk. The Zerivax vaccine builds immunity in advance.

Sepsis

In-game: Blood Poisoning
Cause

Infection entering an open wound — zombie scratch, knife wound, or gunshot, with the odds rising sharply if you handle the wound with dirty hands (or use unsterile sutures).

Symptoms

Health deteriorates gradually if left untreated.

Treatment

Flemoclav or Imipenem (ampoules), Amoxiclav or Topoisomerase (injectors) — all level 3.

Prevent

Disinfect hands and instruments before touching any open wound.

Rabies

Cause

Bites from infected animals such as wolves.

Progression

No immediate symptoms. Stage 1: mild temperature swings and weakness, easy to miss. Stage 2: severe nausea/vomiting that stops you eating or drinking. Stage 3: temperature spike, rapid health loss, exhaustion — fatal without treatment.

Treatment

Rabinoline (level 2 ampoule), Rifampicin (level 3 ampoule), Rabinucoline (level 3 injector). Treat early — waiting for stage 3 is a bad bet.

Prevent

The Rabivax vaccine stops the disease from progressing if taken before infection.

// SYMPTOMS

Pain & Overdose

Two mechanics that cut across every injury and illness above.

Pain

Cause

Any injury — intensity scales with how severe the trauma is.

Recovery

Fades on its own over time.

Treatment

Painkillers of the matching level or higher speed relief: Codeine, Analgin, Nimesulide, Paracetamol, Ibuprofen, Nurofen, Noopept, or Ketarol at level 1; Novacaine or Propital at level 2; Morphinocin, Hexobarbital, Reanimatal, Morphine, or Promidol at level 3.

Overdose

Cause

Taking in too much medication or toxic substance — every med on this page carries an overdose value, and stacking doses adds up.

Stage 1

Nausea and hallucinations, loss of clarity.

Stage 2

Worsens further — possible loss of consciousness, leaving you vulnerable.

Stage 3

Critical health deterioration — this stage is life-threatening.

Treatment

Toxins clear gradually on their own. Drinking water helps flush the system.

Note

Don't chain doses of the same medicine back to back — space treatments out and only escalate to the next tier if the lower one isn't enough.

// EMOTIONAL STATES

Sleep & Mental Health

TerjeMedicine tracks fatigue and psychological stress as real survival stats, not just flavor.

Sleep

Mechanic

Sleep is now tracked like food and water. Yellow indicator: yawning and gradually worsening vision. Red indicator: risk of passing out from exhaustion.

Requirements

Sleep is impossible while freezing, starving, or suffering a stage 2+ illness. Warm places — fireplaces, tents, houses, sleeping bags — are ideal.

Benefit

Sleeping rapidly restores health and blood, and gives a chance to shake off mild illnesses like a stage 1 flu. Energy drinks (MadBull, Gang, Yaguar, PrayBoy, 6Energy) temporarily push back fatigue when you can't afford to sleep.

Mental Health

Cause

Extreme stress: zombie attacks, prolonged exposure to Psionic Zones, or eating human meat.

Symptoms

Nervous breakdowns, hallucinations, panic, and disorientation — all make survival and interacting with the world harder.

Treatment

Antidepressants stabilize the psyche: Adepress, Agteminol, or Venlafaxine (tablets); Actaparoxetine, Amitriptyline, or Metralindole (ampoules); Amfitalicyne (injector).

Psionic Zones

Danger

Areas with high psychoactive effects. The closer you are to the zone's center, the stronger the effect — panic, hallucinations, loss of control. If mental state hits zero, the character can take unpredictable, potentially fatal actions.

Protection

Antidepressants stabilize mental state while inside. A PSI Helm gives roughly 95% protection.

// PROPHYLAXIS AND TREATMENT

Prevention: Vaccines & Disinfection

The two habits that stop most of the diseases above before they ever start.

Vaccines

What they do

Build immunity to a specific disease in advance, cutting infection risk significantly. Best used before you go somewhere risky, and re-applied when immunity wears off.

Options

Vaxicam (against Cold), Zerivax (against Zombie Virus), Rabivax (against Rabies).

Disinfection

In-game buff: Disinfected Hands
Why it matters

Mandatory for sterility. Dirty medical items and unwashed hands are the main path to wound infection and sepsis, especially before any field medical procedure.

Items

Alcoholic Tincture, Disinfectant Spray.

Note

Syringes used with ampoules become contaminated after every injection — disinfect with alcohol or boil in a pot over a fire before reuse.
