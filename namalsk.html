// WHAT AWAITS YOU

Built Different

Every system on NOCTURNIA is tuned to make survival feel brutal, rewarding, and genuinely sci-fi.

🤖

AI Companion

Equip a companion armband to summon an AI buddy that fights alongside you. Solo players finally have backup — if they can keep it alive.

☠️

Deadly Enemies

Mutants, tweaked infected, and Thralls that drop gas on death. Every encounter is a risk assessment. Overconfidence gets you killed.

🚀

Sci-Fi World

Spaceships, CBD Stargate Teleporters, and AI airdrops scattered across Namalsk. The lore is baked into the landscape.

⚔️

Deep Gear System

20+ armor sets, custom weapons, BallerZ Gear, Forward Operator Gear, and DieselGear. Progression that actually feels earned.

🏗️

Advanced Crafting

3D Printer, AgricultureCore, DrugsPLUS, CannabisPlus, DayZOresAndGems — build and craft your way to endgame.

🛒

Multiple Traders

DayZ Expansion traders plus BetterVendingMachines and Paragon Storage. A real economy to support your grind.

💊

Medical Depth

AdditionalMedicSupplies and AMS & Inedia Pain Compatibility add real stakes to injury. Getting hurt matters here.

🏠

Base Security

Underground bases available through the trader — furnish and arrange them however you want. Underground bases keep things off ground level, which means less lag for everyone on the server.

💎

No Pay-to-Win

Donor gear options exist — but every single item is obtainable through gameplay. Money helps, grind does the same.
